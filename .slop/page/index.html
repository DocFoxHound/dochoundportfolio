How a job runs

  1. Brief. You tell me the game, the mood and where the art will be used.
  2. Thumbnails. Three quick value sketches. You pick one.
  3. Rough. Colour and composition locked, one round of changes.
  4. Final. Full paint, one more round of changes, then the files.

No AI. Every piece here, and everything I make for you, is drawn by hand.

Delivered as PNG at full size plus a web copy. Off-palette colours on request, but red and bone is the house style.
